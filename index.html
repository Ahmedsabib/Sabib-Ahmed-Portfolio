I am Sabib Ahmed, a Computer Science graduate from Sylhet Engineering College (affiliated with SUST, Bangladesh). You can reach me at sabibahmed40@gmail.com. You can find my CV here.

I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities to advance explainable, efficient, and trustworthy AI for real-world healthcare and computer vision applications. My research experience spans medical image classification, segmentation, generative AI, and real-time visual detection, with a focus on developing robust deep learning models using Vision Transformers, diffusion models, and parameter-efficient learning techniques. My research interests lie at the intersection of computer vision, medical imaging, explainable AI, multimodal learning, and vision-language models. I aspire to develop deployable AI frameworks that integrate transformer architectures, generative modeling, and interpretable learning to build reliable and impactful intelligent systems.

📣📣📣 I am actively seeking Ph.D. opportunities for Spring 2027/Fall 2027.
